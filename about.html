Introduction

Hi, I'm Aldi Saputra Wahyudi, widely known online as Aldisaw. I'm a cybersecurity professional with over six years of experience in security testing, vulnerability research, and addressing a range of information security challenges. Outside my professional work, I also enjoy creating digital content and sharing experiences.

Professional Background & Academic Journey

My work focuses on offensive security, particularly application security, penetration testing, and vulnerability research. With over six years of experience in security testing, I have worked across web applications, APIs, and mobile platforms, alongside independent vulnerability research and responsible disclosure.

An important milestone in my early cybersecurity journey was participating in the Born to Protect Challenge (2018), a Capture The Flag (CTF) competition where I achieved Top 5 in Bandung and Top 100 nationally in Indonesia. Through this competition, I also had the opportunity to receive Certified Ethical Hacker (CEH) training and earn the Certified Network Defender (CND) certification from EC-Council. This experience strengthened my technical foundation and motivated me to pursue cybersecurity further.

I am currently pursuing a Master's degree in Informatics, further strengthening my expertise in information security management, cyber forensics, and secure software engineering. This academic journey allows me to deepen my understanding of security practices and explore new approaches to identifying, analyzing, and addressing security risks.

Technical Expertise

  • Application & API Security Threat modeling, OWASP Top 10 assessments, authentication & authorization testing, and secure code review.
  • Mobile Application Security Android penetration testing, reverse engineering, dynamic runtime hooking (Frida, Magisk, Zygisk, JsHook), and OWASP MASVS/MASTG.
  • Vulnerability Research & Bug Bounty Independent security research, root-cause analysis, and coordinated disclosure (including CVE-2023-27636).
  • Security Automation & Scripting Developing Python tooling and multi-instance testing environments to streamline security testing and research analysis.

Creative Background & Personal Interests

Beyond cybersecurity and technology, I also have a creative side. I'm active on Instagram and TikTok, where I create and share digital content, explore creative ideas, and connect with different audiences. Content creation has always been another way for me to express myself and tell stories beyond my technical interests.
